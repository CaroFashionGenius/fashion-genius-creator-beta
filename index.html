From upload to first look.

Start whenever it suits you. Four simple steps take you from your own content to a generated virtual try-on.

01 / MODEL

Add your model.

Choose an existing model or upload a clear, well-lit image.

02 / CLOTHING

Add a garment.

Select clothing from your library or upload the piece you want to try.

03 / CREATE

Generate your look.

Open Try-On Studio, pair model and garment, preview the pairing and generate your result.

04 / EXPLORE

Review. Repeat. Explore.

Review generated looks under Results and experiment with different garments and combinations.
